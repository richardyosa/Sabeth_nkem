About Sabeth Nkem

Sabeth Nkem Nigeria Limited is a trailblazing technical service company incorporated in October 1992, specializing in mechanical, electrical, and civil engineering solutions. We are 100% locally owned and have established ourselves as a trusted partner in Nigeria's industrial landscape.

Our Core Services

General Engineering Design

Primarily mechanical-based design and project management with focus on precision and innovation for industrial applications.

EPCM Services

Comprehensive Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Maintenance services tailored to the cement industry.

Maintenance Services

Annual planned shutdowns and daily preventive maintenance from quarry to packing plant across multiple business units.

Fire Suppression Systems

Procurement Services
