EPCM Services

Sabeth Nkem provides comprehensive Engineering, Procurement, Construction, and Maintenance (EPCM) services tailored to the cement industry.

  • Engineering: General mechanical engineering design, focusing on precise and innovative solutions for industrial applications.
  • Procurement: Sourcing high-quality materials, equipment, and components to ensure project efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
  • Construction: Fabrication, installation, and commissioning of systems such as conveyors, silos, elevators, and alternative fuel plants.
  • Maintenance: Annual shutdown planning, daily preventive maintenance, and ongoing support to optimize plant uptime and performance.

Our EPCM approach ensures seamless project execution from concept to operation, with a focus on safety, quality, and sustainability.

Fire Suppression Systems

We specialize in the design, installation, commissioning, and maintenance of automatic fire suppression systems (water and gas-based, including FM200).

  • Installed at Ewekoro and Mfamosing alternative fuel plants.
  • FM200 gas suppression at Ewekoro substation.
  • Firefighting systems across Lafarge business units.
